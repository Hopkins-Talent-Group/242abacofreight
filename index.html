What can't you fly?
Hazardous materials, propane, fuels and anything BAT/FAA restricted. When in doubt, WhatsApp us a photo of the item — we'll confirm before you drive over.

We prepare the customs paperwork for every load. Bahamian duty (if any) is assessed on arrival and settled at pickup at the Marsh Harbour counter. [Process detail placeholder — confirm with owner.]
